# Absurdities and contradictions of my career in crypto

- In October 2021, I had zero experience with coding, so I started a three-month JavaScript/Solidity bootcamp. Less than four years later, I found myself [trying to solve](https://machina-io.com/) the hardest problem in cryptography, having conversations with legends such as Dan Boneh or Amit Sahai. 
- Still cannot explain why blockchains and Ethereum make sense. I sense it has something to do with it being a [globally verifiable time-stamp machine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D7euXlg7ouQ&feature=youtu.be). When people ask me a reason to be bullish on crypto, I always answer "the density of visionary talents". They will figure it out.
- Across the various [products](https://github.com/enricobottazzi) and [research](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=q794hZIAAAAJ) that I built, none of them has been used in production or had a single paying user. The closest I got is with *Greco*, which was forked and improved by [Enclave](https://github.com/gnosisguild/greco?tab=readme-ov-file), which will eventually be used in their mainnet. This may be the only one that worked because it has a cool and memorable name.
- Ended up having a job as a cryptography researcher at the Ethereum Foundation, even though I haven't attended even a single class in linear algebra at Uni.
- Made less than 20 onchain transactions in the last three years. The only transaction that wasn't to pay a friend was the renewal of my ENS domain name `enrico.eth`. I still remember the day in 2021 when I spent around $500 to buy that domain, thinking how dumb it was. Turned out to be great for shilling how OG I am, even if I am not.
- Landed my first job at Polygon ID thanks to a [tutorial](https://github.com/enricobottazzi/ZKverse) on circom I wrote that went viral after Jordi reposted it on Twitter (now that I look at the [post](https://x.com/backaes/status/1497484212475211780?s=46) again, it doesn't look so viral to me, but at the time it felt viral). While writing the tutorial, I had no idea whatsoever about Circom and zero-knowledge proof. I literally transcribed Jordi's words word-for-word from a video presentation I found online. The four hours I spent writing such a tutorial had probably the highest ROI of my life.
- Found a [critical bug](https://www.leku.blog/posts/binance_vuln.html) in the proof of solvency implementation of Binance that scared the shit out of them. The bug demonstrated that they could *potentially* inflate their assets to prove they were solvent if they weren't. I dreamt that it would stir people's consciences, but nothing really happened. At least, I now realize they [fixed it](https://github.com/binance/zkmerkle-proof-of-solvency/blob/main/docs/updated_proof_of_solvency_to_mitigate_dummy_user_attack.md).
- One thing that I often look back at when I need some ego boost is the machina iO [website](https://machina-io.com/) I developed. These subtle animations and the gorgeous minimalist font feel more beautiful than any paper or protocol I designed.
- Less than a week after I started my first crypto job at Polygon ID, I joined my colleagues at Devconnect Amsterdam 2022. My team was invited to join a panel on digital identity happening on Saturday, one day after the conference ended. Since everyone, except for me, had already left Amsterdam, they chose me to join the panel! They told me about it just a few hours before the panel started. I panicked and went for a run to heal the anxiety. Eventually, it went well. I soon learnt that during the panel, no one actually listens to the answers, so when in doubt, you can answer a different question. Also, I looked much more professional back then. ![DAF10639-4623-49F9-ACFE-8C7EFB348BD7](https://hackmd.io/_uploads/BJioOy0ZZg.jpg)
- Still haven't learned how to use git. Instead, I use [Github desktop](https://github.com/apps/desktop) (highly recommended!).
- Worked for more than 3 years on privacy technology, but still accept any cookie that I am presented with for the sake of easy UX.
- In 2017, my mom gifted me a jersey from Japanese football star Honda after her trip to Japan. Eight years later, I found myself presenting at Devcon 2024 in Tokyo right after him (he is now a crypto VC). Not sure whether to interpret it as a personal success or as Honda's downfall
![PHOTO-2025-11-27-18-13-59](https://hackmd.io/_uploads/ryDY5yAWWx.jpg)
- Up to date, the coolest application of privacy technology that I found is [multilateral trade-credit set-off](https://cofi.informal.systems/FAQ.html). The nice feature of this application is that privacy actually enhances the economic benefits for the participants. I wrote a [paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/2037) on that, while [cycles.money](http://cycles.money/) brought this concept to production. I wish there existed an analysis on how privacy influences outcomes in [non-cooperative games](https://medium.com/@virgilgr/ethereum-is-game-changing-technology-literally-d67e01a01cf8). 
- In almost four years of working in crypto, I have had 113 flights, accounting for over 17 days in the air
